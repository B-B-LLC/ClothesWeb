HOW WE WORK

Principles we ship with

No ads, no tracking

Our apps don’t show ads, don’t sell your data and don’t follow you across other apps or websites.

Consent before AI

Photos are sent for AI analysis only after you agree, and they are never used to train AI models.

Your data, your call

Export everything in one file or delete your account from inside the app, at any time.

People answer

Support email is read and answered by the people who build the app, usually within two business days.
