About Shinify LLC

We are an independent software company that designs, builds and operates mobile apps for iPhone and Android.

Who we are

Shinify LLC is a limited liability company organized in Wyoming, United States, in 2024. We make consumer apps for everyday decisions: small, focused tools that are pleasant to use and honest about the data they need.

Our first app is Clothes. It answers a question many of us ask every morning, “what should I wear today?”, from your own wardrobe and the day’s weather.

We do the whole job ourselves: product design, engineering, publishing on the App Store and Google Play, running the servers and answering support email. Shinify LLC is the publisher of our apps and the data controller for the personal data processed through them.

How we build

  • On your device first: the Clothes wardrobe lives on your phone and opens without a connection.
  • Clear consent: photos go to an AI provider only after you agree, and never to train models.
  • No advertising business: no ads, no data sales, no cross-app tracking.
  • Real deletion: account deletion and data export are built into the app.
