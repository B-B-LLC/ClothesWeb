HOW IT WORKS

Upload once, use every day

1

Photograph

Shoot your pieces or pick them from your photo library. Tagging runs in the background while you keep adding.

2

Check the suggestion

The day’s weather and your first suggestion are waiting on Home. Not feeling it? Move on to the next one.

3

Score and share

Get a score for the outfit you wore and share your score card anywhere you like.

DETAILS

Small things that matter

Works offline

Your wardrobe is stored on your phone. Your saved pieces and score results are there even without an internet connection.

Tuned to you

Tell it you were cold and suggestions get warmer; tell it you were too warm and they get lighter. Reset any time.

Approximate location is enough

The weather needs only your approximate location, and only while you’re using the app. You can also pick your city by hand.
